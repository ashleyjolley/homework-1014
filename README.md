Description:

This repository contains 10 Python exercises for DATA 4000. The programs practice Python programming concepts using business-related examples.

Exercises:

Retail Checkout Simulation (checkout.py) – Collects item prices and calculates the total, average, and number of items purchased.

Market Survey Analyzer (survey.py) – Counts customer preferences and calculates product market shares.

Expense Report Categorizer (expenses.py) – Calculates expense totals by category and the overall total.

Sales Commission Calculator (commissions.py) – Calculates employee commissions and ranks employees by commission earned.

Stock Portfolio Tracker (portfolio.py) – Calculates individual stock values and total portfolio value.

Bank Loan Repayment Simulator (loan_simulator.py) – Simulates monthly loan repayment and calculates the number of months needed to pay off the loan.

Simple Supply Chain Tracker (supply_chain.py) – Calculates total inventory across multiple warehouses.

Customer Loyalty Tiers (loyalty_tiers.py) – Assigns customer loyalty tiers and counts customers in each tier.

Business Growth Projection (growth_projection.py) – Projects annual revenue growth over 10 years.

Startup Pitch Deck Visualizer (pitch_chart.py) – Displays a simple ASCII bar chart of projected revenue.

Concepts Practiced:

Variables and data types

Input and output

Arithmetic operations

Conditional statements

Functions

While loops and for loops

Lists and dictionaries

Nested loops

Basic business calculations
